    Refund & Digital Delivery Policy

    Need a refund? Jump to how to request one ↓

    1. Instant digital product

    A VinApex report is a digital product. It is generated from the VIN you enter and delivered online (and to your email) immediately after payment. You can open it as soon as your payment completes.

    2. All sales are final once delivered

    The full content of a report is available the moment it is delivered and cannot be returned. For that reason we do not offer refunds once a report has been delivered or opened. This includes cases where you changed your mind, where the vehicle has since been bought, sold or traded, or where you used the report to negotiate a price.

    3. When we do refund you in full

    • The report could not be generated or delivered.
    • You were charged more than once for the same order.
    • The report was generated for a different VIN than the one you entered, because of an error on our side.

    Please check the VIN carefully before you pay. A report ordered for a VIN that was typed incorrectly cannot be refunded.

    4. Data limits

    Reports are compiled from third-party sources. No report covers every vehicle or every event, and some records may be missing or delayed. A report is information to support your decision, not a guarantee of a vehicle’s condition, value or history, and it does not replace a physical inspection.

    5. Payment records

    By completing a purchase you confirm that you are the authorized holder of the payment method and that you received the report. We keep records of your order, your acceptance of these terms, and the delivery of and access to your report, and we may provide them to our payment provider if a payment is disputed.

    6. How to request a refund

    If you believe your order qualifies under section 3, please contact us within 7 days of your purchase. Here is how the process works:

    1. Send us your request. Email support@vinapex.com from the address you used at checkout, or fill in the request form using the button below. You will need your order email, the VIN, and a short explanation of what went wrong.
    2. We review it. A member of our team checks your order, the delivery record and the report, and decides whether the order is eligible. We reply by email, normally within 2 business days.
    3. If approved, we refund you. The money goes back to the original payment method. After we issue it, your bank or card provider may need 3–5 business days to show it on your statement. Access to the refunded report is then closed.
    4. If we cannot approve it, we will explain why. If you still disagree, reply to our email so we can look again before you contact your bank or payment provider.
